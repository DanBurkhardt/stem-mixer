STEM◆MIXER  ·  QUICK GUIDE

How to use Stem Mixer

Ten things to know. The numbers on the picture match the list.

  1. 1
    Open a song
    Press Open stem folder and pick the song's folder. If the browser says “upload”, nothing leaves your computer.
  2. 2
    Play
    Press Play or the space bar. The left and right arrow keys skip 5 seconds.
  3. 3
    Count-in
    Pick how many clicks you get before the music starts: 1 to 8, or Off.
  4. 4
    Mute your part
    MUTE silences a track so you can play that part yourself. SOLO plays only that track.
  5. 5
    Balance the mix
    Every track has a volume slider and a left/right pan knob. Double-click either one to reset it.
  6. 6
    Loop a hard section
    Drag along the bar above the tracks to mark a section, then switch on Repeat to hear it again and again.
  7. 7
    Zoom
    Zoom in to see the sound up close and place a loop exactly. FIT shows the whole song.
  8. 8
    Click track
    Unmute CLICK for a metronome that follows the recording. Ticking twice too fast or too slow? Press ÷2 or ×2.
  9. 9
    Rename a track
    Click a track's name to change it, for example OTHER to KEYS. The files themselves are not changed.
  10. 10
    Save your setup
    Save settings keeps your mix for this song. Next time you open it, the page offers to bring it back.
STEM◆MIXER  ·  HOW IT WORKS

From playlist to practice

How a Spotify playlist turns into songs you can play along with, one instrument at a time.

ONE PERSON DOES THIS, ONCE PER SETLIST
STEP 1

Pick the songs

Build a Spotify playlist of the covers the band is learning.

STEP 2

Find each song

Give the playlist to Claude. It finds the studio version of every track on YouTube and lists the links.

STEP 3

Save the audio

A free download tool called yt-dlp saves the sound of each video as an MP3 file.

STEP 4

Split the band apart

StemRoller, a free app, separates each song into its parts using its 6-channel setting.

EVERYONE IN THE BAND
STEP 5

Practice

Open the song's folder in Stem Mixer, mute your own part and play along.

WHAT STEP 4 MAKES FOR EVERY SONG
VOCALS
DRUMS
BASS
GUITAR
PIANO
OTHER
INSTRUMENTAL
ORIGINAL

Each part is its own audio file. In Stem Mixer every file becomes a track with its own volume, pan, mute and solo. Instrumental and Original are the whole song, so they start muted.

Good to know. The split is done by software, so a little of one instrument can leak into another. If a track has the wrong label, click its name in the mixer and rename it.

STEM◆MIXER  ·  PIPELINE, FOR ENGINEERS

Playlist → stems → browser mixer

Five stages, mostly off-the-shelf tools. The only custom code is the last stage: one static HTML file.

STAGE 1 · INPUT

Spotify playlist

A playlist URL. It defines the ordered track list: artist and title for every song.

OUT → track list
STAGE 2 · RESOLVE

Claude + web search

Looks up the studio version of each track on YouTube. Returns one watch URL per track, which gets a quick human check.

OUT → watch URLs
STAGE 3 · FETCH

yt-dlp in Docker

Extracts audio only and converts it to MP3 with ffmpeg at best VBR quality. One file per track, numbered in playlist order.

OUT → MP3 files
STAGE 4 · SEPARATE

StemRoller

Demucs-based desktop app. The 6-channel model writes vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and other, plus instrumental and original, as stereo WAV.

OUT → WAV stems
STAGE 5 · MIX

stem-mixer.html

One static HTML file. No server, no build, no dependencies. Reads the stem folder locally and plays it with the Web Audio API.

OUT → speakers

INSIDE STAGE 5 · WHAT THE PAGE DOES WITH A STEM FOLDER

Folder picker

A webkitdirectory file input returns File objects. Nothing is uploaded.

Decode

decodeAudioData turns every file into an AudioBuffer in memory.

Sources

One buffer source node per stem, all started at the same clock time.

Gain

Per-track fader. Mute and solo drive this gain to zero.

StereoPanner

Per-track pan, from hard left to hard right.

Master gain

Sums every track and feeds the speakers.

ALONGSIDE PLAYBACK

Waveforms

Min and max peaks are precomputed at several resolutions and drawn on a canvas, only for the visible part of the timeline.

Beat tracker

Onset envelope from the drum stem, a tempo path that is allowed to drift, then dynamic-programming beat tracking. Runs once per song.

Click and count-in

Clicks are short buffers scheduled on the audio clock along the beat grid. Loops are scheduled a few seconds ahead, pass by pass.

Saved settings

Volume, pan, mute, solo, track names, play range and tempo fixes go in a cookie or localStorage, keyed by a hash of the file names.
